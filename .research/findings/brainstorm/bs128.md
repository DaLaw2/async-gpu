## Q2: Which medium-priority story to activate next?

**gpu-dyn-dispatch** (lang-complete). Rationale:

  patterns still maturing, and whole-program-partition which depends on auto-parallel).
- Directly advances the lang-complete epic's north star ("every Rust feature works").

is complete, and Vec<f32> transparency is a flagship user-facing improvement.

## Q3: New stories or features from recent work?

**panic-abort-path** feature (not a new story — attach to gpu-panic or create a
follow-on story). bs127 identified the gap: `process::abort()` on GPU executes
`trap;` without calling `set_warp_trapped()` or `write_panic_to_result()`, causing
BlockScope::join_all() to spin forever. The patched std has ~10 `process::abort()`
call sites in panicking.rs alone. This is a real reliability issue.

**Recommendation**: Do NOT create it now. The gap is real but rare (only triggers on
double-panic or panic-in-panic-hook). Track it as a known issue. Create the feature
when gpu-dyn-dispatch is underway to avoid scope creep on the freshly-completed story.
